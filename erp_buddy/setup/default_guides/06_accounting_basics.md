# Accounting basics

## Chart of Accounts
- Accounts > Chart of Accounts: each company's account tree (assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses).

## Main documents
- **Sales Invoice**: records revenue and the receivable.
- **Purchase Invoice**: records the expense or stock and the payable.
- **Payment Entry**: receive from a customer (Receive), pay a supplier (Pay), or move money between accounts (Internal Transfer).
- **Journal Entry**: general entries (adjustments, depreciation, other postings).

## Periods
- **Fiscal Year**: the financial year. **Accounting Period** locks posting for a period.

## Reports
- **General Ledger**, **Trial Balance**, **Balance Sheet**, **Profit and Loss Statement**, **Accounts Receivable / Payable**.
