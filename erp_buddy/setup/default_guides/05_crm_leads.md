# CRM: leads and opportunities

## Lead
- CRM > Lead > + Add (/app/lead/new). Enter **First Name** or **Company Name**, email, phone, **Source** (Website, Referral...) and **Status** (Lead, Open, Replied, Interested, Converted...).
- Follow-ups can be scheduled with a **ToDo** or an **Event**.

## Conversion
- From a Lead: **Create > Opportunity** when there is a concrete need; **Create > Customer** once they become a customer.
- From an Opportunity: **Create > Quotation** to send a quote.

## Reports
- **Lead Details**, **Sales Funnel**, **Lost Opportunity**.
